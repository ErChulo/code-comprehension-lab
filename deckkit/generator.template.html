Deck Builder

Turns a rederive decision map and your diagrams into one reveal.js deck. Runs here in the browser. No server, no network, no install.

1. Give it your project

The folder is your ProjectN folder: it holds <repo>/.rederive/map.md, diagrams/*.mmd and notes/*.md. Files stay in your browser.

    2. Build

    3. Your deck

    The downloaded file is one self-contained page. Open it from disk. Space = next step. S = speaker notes. O = overview. Add ?print-pdf to the address to print a handout.
